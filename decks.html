FAQ

Deck Building Questions, Answered

How much does it cost to build a deck in Knoxville?
It depends on size, height, material (pressure-treated wood, composite like Trex or TimberTech, or hardwood), railings, stairs, and add-ons such as lighting or a roof. After a free design consultation we provide a written, itemized quote.
Composite or wood — which is better?
Pressure-treated wood costs less up front and looks great when maintained. Composite decking costs more initially but resists rot, fading, and splinters and needs almost no maintenance. We help you compare both based on your budget and how you'll use the space.
Do I need a permit to build a deck?
Most attached decks in the Knoxville area require a permit. StructureWorks handles permitting and builds to IRC code, including proper footings, ledger flashing, and railing height and baluster spacing.
How long does it take to build a deck?
Most residential decks take one to two weeks from start to finish once materials arrive, depending on size and complexity. We'll give you a clear schedule with your quote.
Do you build screened porches and pergolas too?
Yes. Beyond decks we build screened porches, covered porches, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, and full outdoor living spaces.
