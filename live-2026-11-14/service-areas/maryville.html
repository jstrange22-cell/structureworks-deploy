Permits in Maryville & Blount County

Projects in Maryville are permitted through the City of Maryville, Alcoa through the City of Alcoa, and unincorporated areas through Blount County. StructureWorks determines the right authority, pulls the permit, and manages inspections to current IRC/IBC code. See our permit guide for how the process works.
