Financing & Flexible Payment Options

Quality work shouldn't have to wait. We help Knoxville homeowners and agents find a payment plan that fits the project.

Pay at Closing

For qualifying real estate transactions, defer payment until the property closes. No upfront cost for the agent or their client — ideal for inspection repairs that need to happen before the sale.

Project Financing

Flexible financing for larger roofing, deck, and remodel projects so you can move forward now and pay over time. We'll review available options with you as part of your estimate.

Milestone Payments

Clear, milestone-based payment schedules tied to project progress — so you always know what you're paying for and when.

Insurance Coordination

For storm and hail damage, we document the loss and coordinate with your insurance so approved claims cover the work.

Financing FAQ

Common questions about paying for your project.

Do you offer financing for construction projects?
Yes. StructureWorks works with homeowners and real estate agents to find flexible payment structures for roofing, decks, remodels, and inspection repairs, including financing for larger projects.
Can repairs be paid at closing?
For qualifying real estate transactions we offer payment at closing, so there are no upfront costs for the agent or their client during the deal.
How do I find out what I qualify for?
Request a free consultation and let us know you're interested in financing. We'll review options with you as part of your written estimate.
