What Does a General Contractor Do?

A general contractor is the single point of responsibility for your construction project. Rather than hiring and coordinating a dozen separate trades yourself, your GC plans the work, pulls the permits, orders materials, schedules every crew, and keeps the project on budget and to code. At StructureWorks, that means:

  • Planning & estimating — a clear, itemized written scope and price before work starts.
  • Permits & code — we pull the right permits with Knox County / City of Knoxville and build to current IRC/IBC code.
  • Trade coordination — framing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, drywall, and finishes scheduled in the right order.
  • Quality & inspections — we manage inspection hold points and stand behind the work with a workmanship warranty.
  • One point of contact — you always know who to call.
