Quick Answers

Is the estimate free?
Yes — a free on-site design consultation and written quote with no obligation.
What's the difference between concrete and pavers?
Pavers allow for expansion and contraction without cracking and are easier to repair. Concrete is lower cost up front. We'll walk you through both options based on your budget and goals.
Do retaining walls require permits?
Walls over 4 feet typically require a permit in most Knox County jurisdictions. We advise you on what's required and handle it.
How long does a patio installation take?
A typical paver patio (400–600 sq ft) takes 3–5 days depending on prep, drainage needs, and complexity.
