FAQ

AgentWorks Questions, Answered

What is AgentWorks?
AgentWorks is StructureWorks' partner program for real estate agents. You create a free account, submit inspection repairs or construction jobs with photos and the inspection report, and our team reviews, estimates, and schedules the work — so you keep your transaction on track.
How much does it cost to join AgentWorks?
Nothing. An AgentWorks account is free for agents. You only pay for the actual repair or construction work you approve, and we offer pay-at-closing options on qualifying projects.
How fast can I get an estimate?
Most inspection-repair estimates are turned around within 48 hours of submission so you can keep your closing timeline intact.
Can my client pay at closing?
For qualifying projects we offer payment at closing, so there are no upfront costs for the agent or their client during the transaction.
How do I submit a job?
Sign in to your AgentWorks account, add the property and client details, attach photos and the inspection report (PDF), and submit. You can track the status of every job from your dashboard.
