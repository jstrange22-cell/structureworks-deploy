FAQ

Outdoor Living Questions, Answered

What outdoor living services does StructureWorks offer?
SW Exteriors designs and builds custom decks, screened and covered porches, outdoor kitchens, fencing, hardscape and patios, landscape, and artificial turf across Knoxville and East Tennessee — often combined into one seamless outdoor living project.
Do you design the whole backyard or just one piece?
Both. Many clients start with a deck or patio and add an outdoor kitchen, hardscape, fencing, landscape, and turf over time. Because we self-perform and coordinate the trades, we can design and build the entire outdoor space as one project with one point of contact.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. StructureWorks is fully licensed and insured in Tennessee, carries general liability and workers compensation coverage, pulls the required permits, and builds every structure to current code.
How do I get started?
Request a free design consultation. We visit your home, take measurements, talk through how you want to use the space, and follow up with a design and a clear, itemized written quote. Book your consultation →
