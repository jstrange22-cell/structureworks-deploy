FAQ

Roofing Questions, Answered

How much does a new roof cost in Knoxville?
It depends on the size of the roof, the material (architectural asphalt shingles, metal, or other), pitch, and the condition of the decking. After a free on-site inspection we provide a written, itemized estimate so you know exactly what's included before any work begins.
Do you offer free roof inspections?
Yes. We provide a free, no-obligation roof inspection for homeowners in Knoxville and across East Tennessee, including documentation you can share with your insurance company for storm or hail damage claims.
Can you help with insurance claims for storm damage?
We document the damage with photos and a written report, meet with your adjuster when needed, and complete the repair or replacement to code so your re-inspection goes smoothly.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most residential roof replacements are completed in one to three days depending on size and weather. We protect your landscaping, gutters, and siding and complete a full magnetic nail sweep before we leave.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — StructureWorks is fully licensed and insured in Tennessee, carries general liability and workers compensation coverage, and completes all work to current code.
